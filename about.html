Main Motive of this Project

The Smart Weather Assistant was built to provide users with actionable, real-time, hyper-local weather information. Unlike standard apps that only show numbers, this assistant gives insights and advice on weather conditions such as heat, cold, rain, wind, air quality, and pollen, helping users make informed daily decisions.

Features Overview

Users can search for locations by city, district, pincode, or coordinates, get current conditions, forecasts, AQI, and actionable advice. Voice support, unit toggling (°C / °F), and location detection make it more user-friendly. All interactions are designed to be accessible, intuitive, and visually appealing.
